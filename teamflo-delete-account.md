You can delete your Team Flo BJJ account and its data in two ways.

## Option 1: Delete in the app

1. Open the Team Flo BJJ app and sign in.
2. Go to your Profile.
3. Tap **Delete Account** and confirm.

## Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email **YOUR_EMAIL@example.com** from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Team Flo BJJ account." Your account will be deleted within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and login
- Your profile: name, photo, belt ranks, and competition stats
- Your training journal entries
- Your posts, comments, and photos
- Your notification settings and device token

## What may be kept

- Class attendance records may be kept in anonymous form for gym records.
- Backups may hold your data for up to 30 days before it is fully removed.
